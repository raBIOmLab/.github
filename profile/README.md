### 🎓 [Omicator Workshop: From Omics Data to Biological Discovery](https://luma.com/xzio5p3g)

A hands-on, online introduction to functional genomics and multi-omics analysis, taught through two published cancer research studies.

**You'll work through:**
- **RNA dynamics & DNA hypomethylation:** how CRISPR screens and sequencing revealed roles for RNA decapping, splicing, and m6A methylation in sensitivity to decitabine.
- **Chemogenomics & the DNA damage response:** genome-scale CRISPRi screens with PARP, ATR, ATM, DNAPK, and WEE1 inhibitors, leading to a mechanism linking PRDX1, iron homeostasis, and oxidative damage.

**Topics covered:** RNA-seq and differential expression · CRISPR screens · genetic-interaction maps · hit prioritization · pathway analysis · Ribo-seq · multi-omics integration

**The challenge:** with thousands of measurements and hundreds of candidate genes, which would you take to experimental validation, and why?

**Who it's for:** experimental biologists, students, trainees, and early-career researchers. No advanced programming or bioinformatics experience required.
